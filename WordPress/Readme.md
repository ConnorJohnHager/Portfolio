## 🌐 Featured WordPress Projects

### [tommythetourguide.com](https://tommythetourguide.com/ "Tommy the Tour Guide - Midcentury Modern Architecture Tours in Palm Springs")

**Purpose:**
When I first connected with Tommy, his goal was to expand his business’ reach through his first website. He wanted something simple, modern, and easy to navigate for an older audience, with mobile accessibility and a streamlined way to receive client requests while keeping costs low. Since Tommy has limited web development experience, he needed the site to be easy for him to update text on his own, while keeping technical aspects stable and hassle-free, so he could focus on running his tours rather than managing the site.

**Design and Readability:**
The website was designed with a clean, modern layout that emphasizes spacing and hierarchy to maximize readability. Tour information and contact details are easy to find, with each page ending in a clear call to action directing users to the Contact page. I also tested the site with common browser zoom levels to ensure it remained readable and usable for Tommy’s older target audience.

A light tan background reduces eye strain, with a darker tan used to create subtle visual separation between sections. Tommy’s preferred blue, chosen for its vibrancy, was intentionally applied to navigation, buttons, and interactive elements to clearly signal where users can take action. A brown tone distinguishes headers and quotes from body text, while a terracotta accent adds restrained emphasis through dividers and star ratings that complement the blues in the palette. A slate blue footer and image outlines help ground the layout and visually separate content. Muted gray hover and focus states reinforce interactivity without distracting from the overall experience. The overall palette draws inspiration from architectural materials and aligns with Tommy’s established brand.

**Mobile Accessibility:**
Mobile users were a key consideration throughout development. While most of the website was mobile-friendly from the start, the two photography sections on the homepage required adjustments. I used the free Block Visibility plugin to create separate image layouts for desktop and mobile screens. As a result, the design now scales seamlessly across devices, keeping navigation intuitive and ensuring that all content, including images and forms, remains fully accessible on smartphones and tablets.

**Client Intake Form:**
A simple and effective intake form was created using the free Forminator plugin, sending submissions directly to Tommy’s business email. The form collects only the necessary information for quotes and bookings, making it easy for clients to submit requests without overwhelming them or requiring Tommy to navigate WordPress.

**Cost-Effectiveness:**
Tommy had received quotes starting at $1,000 for a site of this size, not including any WordPress fees, which felt high given the request. By leveraging free plugins like Forminator and Block Visibility and only using a Premium plan for customization, the project was completed in under a month at a total cost of $624, with $400 for my labor and the remainder covering the Premium plan and domain ownership for three years. This approach kept the process efficient, affordable, and fully aligned with Tommy’s goals.
